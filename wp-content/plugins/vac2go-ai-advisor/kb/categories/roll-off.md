# Roll-Off

## Summary

The roll-off category covers roll-off hoist trucks that haul boxes, the roll-off boxes themselves, and Vac2Go's two-box roll-off trailers. Box types include open-top boxes, sealed (gasketed) sludge boxes, vacuum-rated boxes a vacuum truck can load directly, and dewatering boxes. Roll-off staging lets a vacuum truck keep working on site while full boxes are hauled away separately, which helps when volume exceeds 20 cubic yards and the disposal site is far away. Free liquids need sealed boxes or dewatering first. Legal road weight, not box volume, usually limits a load. The customer arranges hauling and disposal of full boxes and all waste profiling.

### Group A: Roll-Off, high level

**Q: What is a roll-off: a roll-off hoist truck that hauls boxes, a vacuum unit that discharges into a roll-off box, the boxes themselves, or some combination?**

The roll-off category encompasses both the heavy-duty roll-off hoist trucks (engineered to haul boxes) and the structural roll-off containers/boxes themselves. Ask whether the customer needs the container, the hauling truck, or both.

**Q: What kinds of jobs are roll-off units used for?**

Refinery basin dredging, industrial wastewater treatment plant press-cake storage, hazardous contaminated soil remediation, construction site demolition debris management, and refinery catalyst dump storage.

**Q: Beyond oil & gas and airports, which industries most commonly use them?**

Environmental remediation, heavy industrial manufacturing, oil & gas, and civil marine dredging.

**Q: What roll-off box types and sizes are available: open top, sealed, vacuum-rated, dewatering, sludge boxes?**

Open-top boxes, gasketed leak-proof sealed boxes, vacuum-rated boxes, and dewatering/filtration boxes.

**Q: Are the boxes vacuum-rated, meaning a vacuum truck can load directly into a sealed box?**

Yes. Specialized vacuum-rated boxes can be hooked directly to an industrial vacuum loader or liquid vacuum truck, allowing material to be sucked directly into the sealed container, eliminating intermediate dumping steps.

**Q: Can roll-off boxes hold liquid and sludge, or only solids?**

Open-top boxes can only hold dry solids. Fully gasketed, sealed sludge boxes are required to safely contain free liquids and wet slurries without trailing leaks on public roads.

**Q: How is a roll-off different from an industrial vacuum truck? When does material go into a box rather than the truck's own tank?**

An industrial vacuum truck must leave the site to dump once its internal tank fills up. A roll-off setup allows the vacuum truck to stay on-site and dump continuously into staged roll-off boxes, which are then hauled away independently by a hoist truck, ensuring 100% vacuum runtime.

**Q: Roll-off truck vs. roll-off trailer: when does each apply?**

A roll-off truck is ideal for standard maneuvering and daily highway swaps. A roll-off trailer can haul two boxes simultaneously or carry extreme payloads inside large industrial plants with strict axle weight configurations.

**Q: Is hauling and disposal of full boxes handled, or do I arrange that?**

Customers arrange that.

**Q: What jobs is a roll-off not the right choice for?**

High-vacuum deep suction lifts or transporting volatile, uncontained gaseous chemical streams.

**Q: What are the most common reasons customers rent roll-off equipment?**

Creating a high-volume, static storage buffer for solid or hazardous waste on-site during large-scale plant cleanouts.

### Group D: Material handling: Roll-Off

**Q: What materials can go into a roll-off box?**

Can hold dry construction soil, saturated canal dredge sediment, industrial filter press cake, concrete demolition breakout, and heavy refinery sludges.

**Q: Can boxes hold free liquid, or must material be dewatered or solidified first?**

Saturated material can go into sealed, gasketed boxes. Free-flowing pure liquids should be solidified with sawdust/polymers or placed into dedicated dewatering filtration boxes first.

**Q: Can they hold hazardous or regulated waste? What profiling & manifesting is required, and who handles?**

Yes, highly utilized for hazardous soils. The customer handles all environmental profiling, manifesting, and securing EPA site approval prior to loading.

**Q: Can they hold abrasive or very dense material? At what point does the box exceed legal haul weight?**

Yes, they handle heavy stone and scrap steel easily. However, heavy materials will exceed legal highway haul weight limits long before the box looks physically full.

**Q: Are dewatering boxes with filtration available, and which materials do they suit?**

Yes, equipped with internal fabric or steel mesh screen filters. Saturated sludges are pumped in; water drains out through bottom valves, leaving dry, easily disposable solid cake behind.

**Q: Are there materials that should never go into a roll-off box?**

Highly volatile reactive chemicals, un-stabilized explosive ordnance, or un-gasketed loose liquids in an open-top container.

### Group E: Sizing and "is this the right truck": Roll-Off

**Q: How do I know if I need a roll-off vs. relying on a vacuum truck's onboard tank capacity?**

Choose roll-off staging if your volume exceeds 20 cubic yards and the disposal site is a long distance away, keeping your primary vacuum truck focused on digging rather than driving.

**Q: What governs the sizing decision more often: box volume or legal haul weight?**

Driven heavily by legal road weight limits rather than total volumetric box size.
