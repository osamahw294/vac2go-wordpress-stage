# Water

Draft. Vac2Go has not supplied Q&A for this category; this is built from the water truck literature and Vac2Go's Round 2 answers. Replace or extend when the client's entries arrive.

## Summary

Water trucks transport and spray water and supply it to a job site, for example as a support truck staged to keep a hydro excavator supplied (Vac2Go's Round 2 answers note the customer arranges the water source, including staging a support water truck). Vac2Go's water trucks carry a Load King 4,000 gallon steel tank with a shaft driven pump, front, mid and rear spray valves, and a hose reel. Do not tell customers the trucks carry potable (drinking) water: the manufacturer's photos show "NON POTABLE" decals, and potable use must be confirmed with a rep. Vac2Go's Round 2 answers also say there is no potable-rated tanker in the fleet.
