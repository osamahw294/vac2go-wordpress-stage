# Huber Dominator SS

The stainless steel version of the Huber Dominator liquid vacuum truck, for liquids that need a stainless tank.

## Key specs
- Stainless option in the Dominator literature: 304 or 316L stainless steel tank and wetted parts {src: DominatorPD brochure p.2}

## Not in our literature
- No document describes the Dominator SS as its own model: its tank capacity, pump, certifications and dimensions. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The Huber Dominator card has the Dominator's published figures; say they describe the standard Dominator and the SS build can differ.
- Stainless suits some acids and caustics but not all; compatibility is confirmed with a rep for the specific product.
