# Super Products Supersucker

The Super Products Supersucker is a truck-mounted industrial vacuum loader that conveys solids, liquids, sludges and slurries through suction lines up to 8" in diameter.

## Key specs
- Payload capacity: 18 cu yd (13.8 m3) {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Vacuum system: 8" (20.3 cm) positive displacement, 5800 cfm/28" hg (9854 m3/hr/0.95 bar) {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Vacuum drive: heavy-duty transfer case {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Suction lines: up to 8" in diameter {src: Supersucker brochure p.1}
- Overall length: 35' (10.67 m) approximate {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Overall height: 12'-10" (3.91 m) approximate {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Overall width: 8' (2.44 m) approximate {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Body construction: 1/4" (6.35 mm) steel throughout body and filter chambers {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Dump: one telescopic double-acting cylinder, 51° dump angle; body, baghouses and separator chamber raise together {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Tailgate: full opening, two lift cylinders, hydraulic latching, liquid tight, adjustable latches and hinges {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}
- Filtration: single mode (wet or dry, no changeover); reverse air pulsation cleaning 60 snap-ring acrylic-coated filter bags; one large cyclone {src: Supersucker brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Industrial waste removal, bulk material recovery and environmental cleanups.
- With pneumatic unloading: discharging dry material into a silo, storage hopper, truck or storage container.
- With the Rail Wheel System: track maintenance and cleaning, bulk spills along tracks, remote vacuum jobs away from access roads, emergency response.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (listed as specifications): the vacuum system, filtration, body, dump and tailgate above.
- Optional: stainless steel components, rear worklights, hydraulic booms, high-pressure washdown systems, Pneumatic Unloading, Rail Wheel System. No specs are given for options.
