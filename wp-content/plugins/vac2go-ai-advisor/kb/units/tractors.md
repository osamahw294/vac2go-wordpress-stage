# Tractors

Over-the-road tractors for pulling Vac2Go's tankers and trailers. They connect to tankers and double trailers. {src: Vac2Go Tractor spec reference; vac2go.com}

## Standard and optional
- A hydraulic wet line kit is available, for running trailer-mounted pumps or blowers. {src: Vac2Go Tractor spec reference; vac2go.com}

## Not in our literature
- Make, model, horsepower, GVWR, axle configuration, cab type, fifth-wheel height and dimensions. Each tractor is spec'd per order, so these come from each tractor's build sheet, not a brochure. A Vac2Go rep can confirm them for the tractor the customer would get.

## Notes for the advisor
- General tractor questions (driver qualifications, wet kits, when to rent a tractor instead of using your own) are answered in the Tractor category knowledge.
- The category knowledge says the wet kit is standard; Vac2Go's tractor spec reference and website say it is available. Say a wet kit is available and a rep can confirm whether it comes on a given tractor.
