# Huber SC 1512

The Huber SC 1512 (Lancer SC1512) is a combination sewer cleaner: a high-pressure jetting system and a vacuum system on one truck, with a forward-top-loading debris tank.

## Key specs
- Debris tank: 12 yd3 (2,400 gal.) capacity, carbon steel {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Fresh water: 1,500 gal. fresh-water capacity {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Dump height: 68" {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Dump angle: up to 50° tank dump angle {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Blower: 5,000 CFM and 18" Hg, Hibon TS 56, continuous duty {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}; the brochure highlights state 5,300 CFM and 18" Hg {src: SC Brochure v07 p.2}. Depends on configuration.
- Water pump: triplex, 86 GPM and 2,500 psi, PTO driven {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Jetting hose reel: 1" x 800' jetting hose, 275° rotation, telescoping 18" {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Boom: 28' Reach with 9' Extension, 8" hose, 180° rotation, 45° pivot, tube-in-tube telescoping {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Tank wash: 11 internal washing nozzles {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Discharge: 6" brass manual sliding discharge valve; hydraulically-operated ¾ rear door {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}

## Typical uses
- Stormwater control, sanitary sewer management, utility maintenance, industrial cleaning, hydro excavation.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: wireless remote, control panel in the hose reel enclosure, low water level alarm, vibrator, 3-point boom jet wash, full-length ladder, directional arrow board, LED DOT lighting.
- Standard accessories: 25' water fill hose, 6" x 6' dig tube, 8" x 6' pickup tubes (2) {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Optional: lance kit; cold weather items (glycol, air purge, recirculation, insulated and heated curbside enclosure); sludge pump; handheld spotlight.

## Not in our literature
- Chassis, engine/HP, GVWR/weights, overall dimensions, CDL needs, maximum jetting distance, nozzle types, a water heater, and whether the water tanks are polypropylene or aluminum. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The literature covers the SC1009 and SC1512 together ("Lancer SC1009 & SC1512"). The tank, fresh water and dump height figures above are the second value of each paired listing, matched to the SC1512 only by the order the models are named; no table names the model next to each figure. Say so if a customer is relying on an exact figure, and offer a Vac2Go rep to confirm.
- Blower: the sell sheet (5,000 CFM) and brochure (5,300 CFM) share the same date. Give both and say it depends on configuration. {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1; SC Brochure v07 p.2}
- Boom: the documents do not say whether the 28' includes the 9' extension. {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- The hose reel's 275° and the boom's 180° are different components. {src: SC Sell Sheet v06 p.1}
- Do not call the cyclone "nine cyclones": the spec list says single cyclone design.
