# Guzzler High-Rail

The Guzzler High-Rail has no document of its own. Our only literature is one option paragraph in the Guzzler Classic brochure, which lists the High-Rail System as an offloading option of the Guzzler Classic (CL), for loading or driving over rails.

## Models covered
- The paragraph says the High-Rail System is available in three configurations and describes two of them {src: Guzzler Classic brochure p.6}:
  - High-rail gear added to a standard Guzzler.
  - A fully equipped high-rail cleaning system with a loading boom, hydraulic creep drive and rear-mounted operator chair, where complete operation can be done from a single position while loading or driving over the rails.

## Key specs
- Base unit: built on the Classic; exact specs depend on the unit. See the Guzzler Classic card for the Classic's published figures.
- No High-Rail figures are published {src: Guzzler Classic brochure p.6}

## Typical uses
- Vacuum loading on and along rail lines; the Classic brochure also names railroads among its industries.

## Standard and optional
- Listed under "Offloading Options" for the Guzzler Classic. Which configuration a unit has depends on the unit.

## Not in our literature
- Names and contents of all three configurations, rail gear specs, track gauge, creep drive speed, debris capacity, airflow, vacuum, dimensions, weights, GVWR and chassis. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Do not present the Classic's figures as High-Rail figures. Say the High-Rail is built on the Classic, exact specs depend on the unit, and a rep can confirm.
- The brochure dates from 2014.
