# Huber Berringer Liquid Ring

The Huber Berringer (formerly Keith Huber) is a liquid ring vacuum tank truck that loads by vacuum and unloads by pressurized or gravity discharge, for hazardous and non-hazardous material.

## Models covered
- Berringer (standard): 1180 CFM liquid ring vacuum pump {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Berringer II options: 1820 CFM liquid ring vacuum/pressure pump with deep vacuum capabilities {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Which pump you get depends on the unit.

## Key specs
- Tank: 3300 gallon carbon steel tank with 5/16" shell {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Manway: 20" top manway {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Vacuum system (standard): PTO driven, hydraulically operated vacuum/pressure system {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Discharge (standard): 6" discharge valve for direct pressurized or gravity unloading {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Intake (standard): 4" intake valve with internal standpipe {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Berringer II valves: 6" discharge and 6" intake rear door valves {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Berringer II drive and filtration: pump powered through a splitter transfer case in line with the chassis main driveline; dual self-cleaning cyclones with return to debris tank {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Loading and transporting a variety of hazardous and non-hazardous material, with pressurized tank discharge at the push of a button.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: full opening hydraulically operated rear door, hydraulic tank lift, rear door and tank lift safety prop bars, full length body trays for hose storage, push button controls in a Nema 4 waterproof container, rear work lights, LED running lights, back-up alarm, grounding reel.
- Optional: Berringer II package (above); multiple tank capacities; ASME and DOT 407/412 {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}; Huber Lock rear door; stainless steel tank with wetted stainless parts; high pressure jetting with integrated water compartment; auxiliary transfer pump; top-mounted hydraulically operated 6" boom with 4" hose {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}

## Not in our literature

## Notes for the advisor
- Source is the 2016 Berringer product family brochure (the Berringer was introduced in 1990). It also covers the Berringer PD, whose PD blower figures do not apply here.
- Quote tank size in gallons only. The brochure prints a barrel figure beside the gallons that does not match it; do not convert.
- Whether this unit is the 1180 CFM standard or the 1820 CFM Berringer II is not known. Give both and say it depends on configuration. {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- ASME and DOT are options. Never say this unit has them without confirmation.
