# GapVax HV-56

A truck-mounted hydro excavator from the GapVax HV Series, built for wet or dry vacuum work: digging with high pressure water and vacuuming the spoil into a debris body.

## Models covered
- HV56: the Vac2Go fleet unit. The same literature also covers the HV55, which is a different model and not this unit.

## Key specs
HV56-specific (from the HV56 model description):
- Debris body: "15 cubic yard Debris Body" {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.1}
- Water tank: "1,000 or 1,200 gallon water tank capacities available" {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.1}
- Airflow / vacuum: 5,250 CFM / 28" Hg {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.1}
- Cyclones: six cyclones and filter bags {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.1}

HV Series (series-level; the brochure does not confirm each item for the HV56 specifically):
- Blower: positive displacement, 5,250 CFM, 28" Hg {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Drive: 500 HP transfer case with air-shift engagement {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Water pump: tri-plex, direct-shaft driven via PTO, "19 gpm/2,900 PSI (other options available)" {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Water system: "600-1,600 gallon options" across the series {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Boom: vacuum loading boom with 25' reach, extendable {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Filtration: wet or dry single mode; top loading debris tank with 12" stainless steel floatball shutoff; five or six centrifugal cyclone separators; baghouse with snap-ring filter bags; final stainless screen {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Debris body: dump angle at over 45°; full opening tailgate with field-adjustable locks {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}
- Body access: 20" body access manway {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation and dry vacuum jobs; true wet or dry operation.
- Air excavation is listed as an optional package.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (series-level features): blower and t-case dampening mounts, all steel air duct with cleanout access, full bearing body pivot, heavy-duty electric vibrator, heavy-duty rear splash plate, body and tailgate safety props, 25' reach boom.
- Optional (series-level): dipping boom; stainless steel water and/or debris tanks; heated cabinets; water heater, 400,000 BTU or 575,000 BTU {src: GapVax HV Literature 2026 p.2}; glycol injection; liquid level load indicator; toolbox and tube rack options; auxiliary lift axles; air excavation package; lighting options.

## Not in our literature
- Hose sizes and lengths, boom rotation, overall dimensions, weights/GVWR, chassis make and model, engine, axle configuration, debris body material. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Keep HV56 figures and series-level figures apart. Say "the HV Series literature lists" for anything from the series-level list.
- Water tank: the HV56 is listed at 1,000 or 1,200 gallons; which one a given unit has depends on the unit.
- Water pump, water heater and options vary by build. Do not say a rental unit has a heater, a specific heater rating, or water recirculation; a rep can confirm.
- Printed specs are subject to change without notice.
