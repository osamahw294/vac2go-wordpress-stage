# Guzzler Dense Phase

The Guzzler Dense Phase is the Dense Phase Offloading system on the Guzzler NX air mover. The system combines pressure offloading with vacuum recovery in a closed-loop system, so dry material can be blown back into storage silos, rail cars or other containers.

## Key specs
- Base unit: Guzzler NX air mover with the Dense Phase Offloading system {src: Guzzler Dense Phase sheet p.1}
- Dry material conveyance: up to 120 feet vertically {src: Guzzler Dense Phase sheet p.1}
- Vacuum recovery: returns any carryover back to the tank, closing the loop in the load and unload process {src: Guzzler Dense Phase sheet p.1}

## Typical uses
- Solids and dry bulk powders such as fly ash, through to liquids, slurries and thick heavy sludge.
- Cement or dry bulk powder work.
- Reclaiming, recycling and redistributing material into storage silos, rail cars or other containers.
