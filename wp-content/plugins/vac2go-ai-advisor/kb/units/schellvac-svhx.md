# Schellvac SVHX

The SVHX is SchellVac's line of truck-mounted hydrovacs (hydro excavators) for digging with high pressure water and vacuuming the spoil into a debris body.

## Models covered
- SVHX8, SVHX9, SVHX11, SVHX12 and SVHX14. Which model you get depends on the unit; the literature does not say which one Vac2Go runs.

## Key specs
SVHX11 (midsize):
- PD blower: Robuschi RBDV 125, 3800 CFM, 28" Hg {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Water pump: Pratissoli KG28A, 18.4 GPM, 2900 PSI {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Boom: 23-feet reach with 320 degree rotation {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Boiler: Dynablast HV690F, 690,000 BTU {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Debris tank: 11 cubic yards, two compartments, carbon steel / aluminum, standard hard epoxy lined {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Water capacity: 1000 gallons {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}
- Remote control: wireless / manual; heated valves standard {src: SchellVac booklet p.2}

SVHX8 (urban unit):
- PD blower: Robuschi RBDV 105, 2600 CFM, 28" Hg {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Water pump: Pratissoli T2040, 10.56 GPM, 2900 PSI {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Boom: 20-feet reach with 320 degree rotation {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Boiler: Dynablast HV420F, 420,000 BTU {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Debris tank: 8 cubic yards, two compartments, carbon steel / aluminum, standard hard epoxy lined {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Water capacity: 860 gallons {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}
- Remote control: wireless / manual; heated valves standard {src: SchellVac booklet p.3}

Other models (headline figures only):
- SVHX9: 3800 CFM Robuschi blower; 9 cubic yards debris body; 900 US gallons water; 72" spread tandem rear axles {src: SchellVac booklet p.4}
- SVHX12: 6400 CFM Robuschi blower; 12 cubic yards debris body; 1200 US gallons water; self-steering pusher axle {src: SchellVac booklet p.4}
- SVHX14: 6400 CFM Robuschi blower; 12 - 14 cubic yards debris body; 1000 - 1350 US gallons water; heavy duty tridrive {src: SchellVac booklet p.4}

## Typical uses
- SVHX8: urban jobsites and governmental jobs on narrow city or municipal streets with limited weight allowances; described as shorter and narrower than the other models (no dimensions given).
- SVHX9 and SVHX11: midsize hydrovacs; the SVHX11 is described for city or rural applications, with or without winterization.
- SVHX14: heavy duty work in the fields or on pipelines.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (SVHX8 and SVHX11): hard epoxy lined tank, heated valves.
- Optional: winterization is a configuration choice on the SVHX11. No option lists are given for the other models.

## Not in our literature
- Filtration and cyclones, hose sizes and lengths, overall dimensions, weights/GVWR, chassis make and model, and engine, for every model. Full spec tables (pump, boom, boiler) exist only for the SVHX8 and SVHX11. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Always name the model with a figure, and say which model you get depends on the unit. Ask a rep before quoting a single number as "the" SVHX spec.
- The booklet dates from 2018 and the SVHX8 and SVHX11 may no longer be current models, so specs may be outdated.
- The SVHX14 figures are ranges; quote them as ranges.
- Keep units as printed: "gallons" for the SVHX8 and SVHX11, "US gallons" for the SVHX9, 12 and 14.
- The booklet also covers SchellVac vacuum tank and portable toilet trucks (SVIT, SVPT, SVST). Those are not this unit; do not quote their figures.
