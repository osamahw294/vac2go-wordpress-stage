# GapVax HV-57

A GapVax air mover with wet/dry capability, in the Industrial Vacuum category. It recovers wet or dry debris and bulk material.

## Key specs
- Standard airflow: 5,200 to 5,250 CFM, positive displacement blower {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Upgraded pump: 6,000 to 6,600 CFM {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Vacuum: 27 to 28" Hg {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Drive: 500 HP transfer case with air-shift engagement {src: HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Debris capacity: about 15 to 17 cu yd (usable vs. body) {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Filtration: wet/dry single-mode, 7 cyclone separators, baghouse, 40 to 46 filter bags (part #GV20002), 12" stainless steel floatball shutoff {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2; HV-57 manual}
- Body: 1/4" EXTEN-steel, 45 degree dump, full-opening tailgate with four fail-safe locks, 20" body access manway {src: Phase 1 knowledge base; HV-57 brochures p.2}

## Standard and optional
- Standard: tailgate porthose; quiet air injection and discharge silencers. {src: HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Optional: High Dump (72" dump height with the body down, 61" with the body dumped, 22" rearward travel). {src: HV Series brochure p.1}
- Optional: stainless steel debris body, top-loading hydraulic booms, sound enclosure for the vacuum pump, tailgate splash shield, liquid load indicator, auxiliary lift axles, auger unloading, Hi-Rail system, wet/dry pneumatic blow-off, body pressurized system, washdown system, wireless controls. {src: HV-57 brochures p.2}
- Which options a given Vac2Go unit carries depends on that unit; a Vac2Go rep can confirm.
