# GapVax HV-57

A GapVax air mover with wet/dry capability, in the Industrial Vacuum category. It recovers wet or dry debris and bulk material.

## Key specs
- Standard airflow: 5,200 to 5,250 CFM {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
- Upgraded pump: 6,000 to 6,600 CFM {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
- Vacuum: 27 to 28" Hg {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
- Debris capacity: about 15 to 17 cu yd (usable vs. body) {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
- Filtration: wet/dry single-mode, 7 cyclone separators, baghouse, 40 to 46 filter bags (part #GV20002) {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
- Body: 1/4" EXTEN-steel, 45 degree dump, full-opening tailgate {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}

## Standard and optional
- Optional: High Dump {src: Phase 1 knowledge base}
