# Water Trucks

Water trucks with a Load King 4,000 gallon steel water tank, a shaft driven pump, front, mid and rear spray valves, and a hose reel, for transporting and spraying water.

## Key specs
- Capacity: 4,000 US gallons {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2; Vac2Go Water Truck spec reference (vac2go.com) p.1}
- Tank dimensions: 198" long x 96" wide x 64.75" tall {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Tank: A36 steel shell, domed ends with flange, (3) baffles with 20" crawl hole, modified ellipse shape {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Man-way: 24" round, mesh lift out grate, no lid {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Fill pipe: 2-1/2" pipe, cam groove coupler {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Water pump: 4X3, shaft driven; creek fill suction; no primer pump {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Front spray: (2) independently operated air spray valves {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Mid spray: (1) air spray valve, drive side {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Rear spray: (2) independently operated air spray valves {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Hose reel: manual rewind, 50' x 1-1/2" hose, firemans nozzle {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Hydrant hose: 2-1/2" x 50' {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Suction hose kit: (1) 3" x 25' cam-loc hose with 3" suction strainer {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}
- Ladder: rear mounted, anti-slip rungs {src: Load King 4000 Water Truck brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Transporting and spraying water; filling from a hydrant or by creek fill suction.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: everything listed above, plus DOT/ICC compliant lights and reflectors.
- Optional: a passenger side mid spray valve.

## Not in our literature
- Pump flow (GPM) and pressure, GVWR, weights, overall truck dimensions, chassis make and model, tank lining. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Drinking water: do not tell customers these trucks carry potable (drinking) water. The manufacturer's photos show "NON POTABLE" decals on the tank, and the brochure says nothing about potable use. Any potable use must be confirmed with a rep.
- The tank is available on a variety of chassis brands and models; the chassis on a given truck depends on the unit.
- The Vac2Go reference describes the hose reel as rear mounted and the nozzle as adjustable; the brochure says firemans nozzle. Both describe the same reel.
