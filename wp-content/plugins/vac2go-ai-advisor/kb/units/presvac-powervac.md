# PresVac PowerVac

The PresVac PowerVac is a truck-mounted PD blower wet/dry vacuum loader. It can work as an air mover for dry material or at full vacuum for liquid, sludge and slurry jobs.

## Models covered
The sheet shows three built units; which model you get depends on the unit {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- PowerVac 3800: Hibon SIAV 840 blower; free air 3800 CFM, 3300 @ 18" HG, 1600 @ 27" HG; Carbon Steel SA36 tank; DOT 407/412 code {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- PowerVac 5300: Hibon SIAV 8702 blower; free air 5300 CFM, 4600 @ 18" HG, 2950 @ 27" HG; Stainless Steel 316 With High Polish tank; DOT 407/412 code {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- PowerVac 6400: Robuschi RBDV 145 blower; free air 6200 CFM, 5100 @ 18" HG, 2900 @ 27" HG; Stainless Steel 316 With High Polish tank; Dual TC/DOT 407/412 code {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}

## Key specs
- Series vacuum: Full Vacuum (28HG) {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}
- Tank size (all three units): 16 Cubic YDS, 3250 USG, 2700 IMP {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- Drive (all three units): OMSI Heavy Duty Transfer Case {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- Code tank: ASME, DOT 407/412, TC 407/412, Dual DOT/TC, 35 PSI Max {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}
- Non-code tank: heavy duty construction, relief @14.5 PSI {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}
- Tank material choices: Carbon Steel, SS 304, SS 316, SS 316 High Polish {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}
- Filtration: modular, matched to blower and application, with material knock-out features in the debris tank {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}
- Optional pressure off-load: PV750 Pressure Off-Load, 35 PSI {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- Optional boom: 8" Power Rotation Loading Boom {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- Optional walkway railing: 42" {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}
- Optional top manway: 20" {src: PowerVac sheet p.2}

## Typical uses
- Air mover work on dry material.
- Full vacuum recovery of liquids, sludge and slurry.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: the sheet does not list standard equipment beyond blower, transfer case, tank and filtration.
- Optional: boom, pressure off-load, water pump, sound proofing, heated valves, top manway, rear loading port, tank anti-surge baffle, pressure and pneumatic unloading systems, high pressure water pump, high rail, acoustic enclosure, HEPA filter hook up ports, transfer pump, walkway railing, custom hose trays and tool boxes, two tone paint, tank striping.

## Not in our literature
- Water capacity, water pump rating, hose sizes and lengths, dump or dump height, dimensions, weights, GVWR, chassis make. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The sheet says its specifications refer to the unit pictured, so each model block describes one built example. Quote per-model figures with the model name.
- "Free air" CFM is unrestricted airflow. Airflow under vacuum is lower (for example 2900 @ 27" HG on the 6400). Do not present free air as working performance.
- The model names do not match free air exactly (the 6400 lists 6200 CFM). The series range printed on p.1 is 3500 to 6600 CFM, which none of the three units spans {src: PowerVac sheet p.1}.
- The blower on a given unit may differ from the one pictured.
- Do not promise that a unit is certified for hazardous waste; that depends on the specific tank's current code status. A rep confirms.
- The sheet is undated.
