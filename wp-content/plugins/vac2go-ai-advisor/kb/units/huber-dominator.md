# Huber Dominator

The Huber Dominator (formerly Keith Huber) is a vacuum tank truck for hauling liquids and sludges, with vacuum loading, a full opening hydraulic rear door and a dumping tank.

## Models covered
- Dominator Series IV: a "Tank Forward" design with a liquid cooled or air cooled vacuum pump.
- Dominator PD: a non code tank with a DLDV65 pump package.
- Which configuration you get depends on the unit.

## Key specs
Dominator Series IV:
- Tank: 3000 gallon (70 bbl or 15 cubic yard), single compartment code carbon steel {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}
- Shell / heads: 5/16" shell thickness; 3/8" head thickness {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}
- Vacuum pump: liquid cooled or air cooled vacuum pump options from 320-670 CFM, hydraulically driven {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}
- Intake / discharge: 4" intake valve with internal standpipe; 6" discharge valve {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}
- Manway: 20" top manway with access ladder {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}
- Code: ASME and DOT 412 on the feature list {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}; the cover says it is offered in both non-code and code options {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.1}

Dominator PD:
- Tank: 3300 gallons with a 3% outage (75 barrels or 17 cubic yards) {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}
- Tank build: single compartment non code carbon steel tank, 5/16" shell rated for 15 psi working pressure {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}
- Pump: DLDV65 pump package, PTO driven hydraulic system; four way valve for vacuum loading and pressure discharge {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}
- Intake / discharge: 4" intake valve with internal standpipe; 6" discharge valve {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Hauling liquid loads, including thick heavy solids (PD). No specific industries are named.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (both): anti-surge baffles, independent tank mounting, telescoping tank lift cylinder, stainless steel float ball shut-off, rear liquid level gauge, waterproof controls.
- Optional (Series IV): 304 or 316L stainless wetted parts {src: Dominator Series IV Brochure p.2}; Viton seals; hydraulic vibrator; heated valves; auxiliary transfer pump.
- Optional (PD): tank sizes 3,000 to 4,000 gallon; ASME up to 35 psi MAWP; DOT 407/412; 20" top manway; top mounted 6" boom with a 4" hose {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}
- Optional PD water packages: wash down 10GPM @ 2,000 psi with 200' of 1/2" hose; jetting 35GPM @ 2,000 psi with 200' of 5/8" hose {src: DominatorPD Brochure p.2}

## Notes for the advisor
- Both brochures date from 2016. Never quote a CFM for the PD; its brochure gives none.
- Series IV code status: the feature list says code (ASME and DOT 412) but the cover says non-code and code are offered. Confirm per unit.
- ASME, DOT and stainless are options. Never say this unit has them without confirmation.
