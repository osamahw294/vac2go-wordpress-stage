# Vactor 2100 Plus

The Vactor 2100 Plus is a truck-mounted combination sewer cleaner (vacuum plus jetting), built either as a Fan Model or a Positive Displacement (PD) Model. Our only literature is a 2011 brochure, which leaves out many basic figures.

## Models covered
Which platform you get depends on the unit {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.2, p.4, p.6}
- Fan Model: 38-inch (965 mm) aluminum fan, powered through a step-up transmission with helical gears; has an auxiliary engine (Fan Model only) {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.4, p.5}
- PD Model: chassis-driven PD blower through a 475 hp continuous-duty transfer case; inlet volume up to 5,000 cfm and 18 Hg {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.6}

## Key specs
- Dump: standard 60-inch high dump, unloads into roll-offs up to 5-feet high without a lift or ramp {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.5}
- PD filtration: multi-stage, with stainless steel screen strainers protecting the blower from particles as small as 750 microns {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.6}
- Boom: fully hydraulic, rotating up to 180 degrees {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.4}
- Hose reel: front mounted, rotating, telescopes forward and backward up to 15 inches {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.4}
- Water pump: Jet Rodder hydraulic single piston pump (no gpm or psi stated) {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.5}
- Water tanks: aluminum, modular {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.5}
- Optional water tank capacity: up to 1,500 gal {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.7}
- Optional pump delivery system: up to 120 gpm {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.7}
- Optional hose reel capacity: up to 1,000 ft (305 m) {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.7}
- Optional 5 x 5 boom: telescopes outward five feet and lets the hose move down an additional five feet {src: Vactor 2100 Plus brochure p.7}

## Typical uses
- Sewer cleaning and catch basin or inlet cleaning without moving the truck.
- Unloading debris into roll-offs.
- With the optional HydroExcavation Kit: excavating or locating buried utilities.

## Standard and optional
- Explicitly standard: multi-flow system, 60-inch high dump.
- Described features (not marked optional): IntelliView display, E-stops at operator control points, tool and pipe storage, hydraulic boom, telescoping hose reel, Jet Rodder pump, aluminum water tanks.
- Optional: nozzle storage, tool boxes, enhanced cyclones, wireless e-stop and hose reel controls, Wireless Belly Pack, cold weather recirculation, debris pump-off and flush-out systems, hydraulic door locking, larger water tank, pump delivery system, larger hose reel, accumulator, dual-stage centrifugal compressor, dual boom controls, level wind, Armor Belt hoses, OEM nozzles, HydroExcavation Kit, 5 x 5 boom, hazard lights, arrowboards, backup cameras.

## Not in our literature
- Debris body capacity, standard water tank capacity, water pump gpm and psi, Fan Model cfm and vacuum, jetting hose size, blower make and model, dump angle, chassis, engine, GVWR, dimensions, weights. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- 475 hp is the transfer case rating, not engine horsepower.
- "18 Hg" is printed without "in".
- The brochure dates from 2011; current units likely differ. For any basic figure, say it is not in our literature and a rep can confirm.
- Vac2Go's equipment catalog lists this unit as "Vactor 2100+ / 2100i". Our literature is the 2100 Plus brochure; a rep confirms which version a customer would get.
