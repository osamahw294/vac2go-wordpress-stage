# Huber AM30 HD

The Huber AM30 HD is a truck-mounted industrial vacuum (air mover). No manufacturer document we hold names the AM30 HD. Vac2Go treats it as essentially the same unit as the Huber AM36, so the AM36 literature is the reference below, with the same hedges.

## Key specs
These are AM36 figures, not AM30 HD figures from any document:
- Debris tank: 3,600 gallon tank {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Blower: 5,300 CFM PD Blower {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Filtration: baghouse with (40) 5' long filter bags {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Intake: 8" intake port shown with the standard features {src: AM36_HD p.2}; the older brochure lists 6" intake and discharge ports as standard and an 8" intake as an option {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}. Depends on configuration.
- Discharge: 6" discharge valve {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Dump angle: 45° dump angle {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- HighDump option: 68" HighDump Option {src: AM36_HD p.2}

## Typical uses
- Industrial vacuum work, wet or dry products (AM36 literature). No specific industries are named.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (AM36): silencer, tank safety prop, sludge pump ready, LED lights, backup alarm, grounding reel.
- Optional (AM36): top mounted 22' retractable boom {src: AM36_HD p.2}; water package 10/20 GPM @2,500 psi {src: AM36_HD p.2}, listed in the older brochure as 10 GPM @ 2500 psi and 20 GPM @ 4000 psi {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}. Depends on configuration.

## Not in our literature
- Any AM30 HD specific figure. Also vacuum level (in Hg), blower make/model, chassis, engine, GVWR/weights, dimensions, and whether this unit has HighDump, a boom or water. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Always say these are the AM36's published figures, used because Vac2Go treats the AM30 HD as essentially the same unit. Never present them as confirmed AM30 HD specs.
- Where the two AM36 documents differ (intake, water package pressure), give both and say it depends on configuration.
- For anything the customer needs to rely on, offer a Vac2Go rep to confirm the actual unit.
