# Guzzcavator

A Guzzler truck built to work both as an industrial vacuum loader and as a hydro excavator: high-pressure water to loosen soil, and a positive displacement blower with baghouse filtration to recover wet or dry material. Vac2Go lists it in the Hydro Excavator category.

## Key specs
- Debris body: 16 yd3 payload capacity {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}
- Blower: positive displacement tri-lobe; max airflow range 5,250 cfm; max vacuum range 27 Hg {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}
- Filtration: four stages (debris body, cyclone chamber, baghouse, microstrainer); sixty 70" Dacron filter bags {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.4}
- Bag cleaning: 120 psi pulsation system {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}
- Water tank: 1,300 gallon stainless steel {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.3}
- Water system: rated at 10 gpm @ 2500 psi, hydraulically operated triplex piston pump {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.3, p.5}
- Optional water system: 20 gpm @ 2,500 psi {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.3, p.5}
- Handgun hose: 3/8 in x 75 ft {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}
- Optional water heater: 400,000 or 800,000 BTU/hr {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}
- Hydraulic tank: 50-gallon {src: Guzzcavator brochure p.5}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation and industrial vacuum loading with the same truck, for example digging, cleaning, and recovering wet or dry material at plants and industrial sites.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: wireless remote, ladder, catwalk and railing, E-Stop, body and rear door props, back-up alarm, 8 in air-operated relief valve with wireless control, 4 in manual relief valve.
- Optional: water heater, rear door mounted sludge pump, full opening rear door, 20 gpm water system, cold weather package, air purge, extendable boom, heated cabinet for the water pump.

## Not in our literature
- Chassis, engine, weights, GVWR, vacuum hose diameter and dump angle. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The water system is rated at 10 gpm; 20 gpm applies only to the optional system. Do not quote 20 gpm as standard.
- The brochure dates from 2014, so specs may differ on the unit a customer gets.
- Do not call the telescoping boom standard; the brochure offers a choice of booms.
- The dimension drawing is unlabelled; do not quote a wheelbase or overall dimensions from it.
