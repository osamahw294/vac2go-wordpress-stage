# Huber Berringer PD

The Huber Berringer PD (formerly Keith Huber) is the positive-displacement (PD) blower version of the Berringer vacuum tank truck, for loading and transporting liquids and sludges, hazardous and non-hazardous.

## Key specs
- Tank: 3000-3500 gallon (70-83 bbl) carbon steel tank with 5/16" shell {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Blower: 1600 or 2400 CFM blower with silencer {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Intake: 6" manual intake valve with internal "Y" standpipe {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Loading and transporting a variety of hazardous and non-hazardous material.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: the brochure's PD features list gives only the tank, blower and intake above.
- PD options: Robuschi 85 or 105 blower {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}; vacuum pump exhaust system with 6" camlock hose connection for "down-winding" {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}
- Berringer family options: multiple tank capacities; ASME and DOT 407/412 {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}; Huber Lock rear door; stainless steel tank with wetted stainless parts; high pressure jetting with integrated water compartment; auxiliary transfer pump; top-mounted hydraulically operated 6" boom with 4" hose {src: Berringer-Brochure p.2}

## Not in our literature

## Notes for the advisor
- The only source is the 2016 Berringer product family brochure, which covers the liquid ring Berringer and the Berringer PD together. The liquid ring pump figures in that brochure do not apply to the PD; see the Huber Berringer Liquid Ring card.
- Tank and blower are given as ranges or choices. Say the exact figure depends on the unit's configuration.
- Do not pair the blower CFM choices with the Robuschi blower models; the brochure does not.
- The brochure does not say whether the standard Berringer's rear door, tank lift and lights also apply to the PD.
- ASME and DOT are options. Never say this unit has them without confirmation.
