# Huber Baron HX

The Huber Baron HX (model Baron HX1512) is a truck-mounted hydro excavator: a high-pressure water system to cut the soil and a vacuum system to load it into a dumping debris tank.

## Key specs
- Debris tank: 12 yd3 (2,400 gal.) capacity, carbon steel {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Fresh water: 1,500 gal. fresh-water capacity, polypropylene tanks on both sides of the debris tank {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Blower: Hibon 8702 blower, 5,300 CFM and 27" Hg, continuous duty, shaft driven from transfer case {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- At idle: the blower pulls 3,000 CFM at 18" Hg, or 1,100 CFM at 27" Hg {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Water pump: triplex, 20 GPM and 3,000 psi, hydraulically driven, variable flow and pressure {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Hose reel: ½" x 100' high-pressure hose, pneumatic rewind {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Boom: 24' Reach with 6' Extension, 8" hose, 330° hydraulic rotation, top-loading at rear of tank {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Dump: up to 50° tank dump angle, 60" dump height {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Discharge: 6" brass manual sliding discharge valve; hydraulically-operated ¾ rear door {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Cyclone: single cyclone design with catch can and cleanout door, which the manufacturer markets as a 9-stage (PureFlow9) cyclone {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}

## Typical uses
- Daylighting, trenching, potholing and cleanup.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: Smart Idle air flow, wireless remote (boom, water pump, blower, engine speed, E-Stop), curbside control panel, low water level alarm, tank vibrator, LED DOT lighting.
- Standard accessories: 25' water fill hose, 6" x 6' dig tube, 8" x 6' pickup tubes (2), lance kit with ½" x 72" and ½" x 48" digging lances {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}
- Optional: Cold Weather Package with 700K BTU streetside burner and glycol system {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}; Air Excavation Package, 185 CFM at 150 psi with ¾" x 75' air hose {src: Baron HX1512 2026 p.3}; sludge pump; directional arrow board; handheld spotlight.

## Not in our literature
- Chassis make/model, engine/horsepower, GVWR/weights, overall dimensions, CDL requirement, fuel type, maximum dig depth, and a water heater as standard (heat comes only with the optional Cold Weather Package). A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The three Huber documents (2025 brochure and sell sheet, 2026 combined brochure) agree on the main figures; the idle figures appear only in the 2026 document.
- Boom reach: quote "24' reach with 6' extension". A table in the sell sheet lists 30 (ft.) {src: HydroEx Sell Sheet v05 p.2}; the documents never say whether that is the 24' plus the extension.
- Do not say it has nine cyclones. The spec list describes a single cyclone.
- The water pump is listed as hydraulically driven; quote that.
