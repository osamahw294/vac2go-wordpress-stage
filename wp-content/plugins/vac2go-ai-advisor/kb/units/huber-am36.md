# Huber AM36

The Huber AM36 (formerly Keith Huber) is a truck-mounted industrial vacuum (air mover) with a PD blower, cyclone and baghouse filtration, and a dumping debris tank. The literature says it handles wet or dry products.

## Models covered
- AM36 with standard rear dump.
- AM36 with the optional HighDump (a hydraulically lifted high dump tank with outriggers). Which one you get depends on the unit.

## Key specs
- Debris tank: 3,600 gallon tank {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Tank build (older brochure): 3600 gallon carbon steel tank with 1/4" shell {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- Blower: 5,300 CFM PD Blower {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Filtration: baghouse with (40) 5' long filter bags {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Cyclone: Huber filtration cyclone with 6" baghouse and cyclone cleanout ports {src: AM36_HD p.2}; the older brochure calls it a centrifugal 9-stage cyclone {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- Intake: 8" intake port shown with the standard features {src: AM36_HD p.2}; the older brochure lists 6" intake and discharge ports as standard and an 8" intake as an option {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}. Depends on configuration.
- Discharge: 6" discharge valve {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- Dump angle: 45° dump angle {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- HighDump option: 68" HighDump Option, an optional hydraulically lifted high dump tank {src: AM36_HD p.2}

## Typical uses
- Industrial vacuum work, wet or dry products. The literature names no specific industries.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: silencer, integrated tank safety prop, sludge pump ready, foldable hand rail, simple controls, LED rear work and running lights, backup alarm, grounding reel {src: AM36_HD p.2}; hydraulically operated rear door with integrated valves {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- Optional: top mounted 22' retractable boom {src: AM36_HD p.2}; auxiliary sludge pump; stinger/pickup tubes; tank mounted hydraulic or electric vibrator {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}
- Optional water package: 10/20 GPM @2,500 psi with 300-600 gallon water capacity {src: AM36_HD p.2}; the older brochure lists 10 GPM @ 2500 psi and 20 GPM @ 4000 psi {src: Huber Air Mover Brochure p.2}. Depends on configuration.

## Not in our literature
- Vacuum level (in Hg), blower make/model, hose sizes and lengths, chassis, engine, GVWR/weights, dimensions, payload, the dump height of the standard version, and whether a given rental unit has HighDump, a boom or water. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Two documents: AM36_HD (2024, newest) and the Huber Air Mover Brochure (2017). Where they differ (intake size, water package pressure), give both and say it depends on the unit's configuration.
- The boom is an option. Do not say a unit has a boom unless a rep confirms it.
- The document does not say what the 68" in "68" HighDump" measures. Do not call it a dump height. {src: AM36_HD p.2}
- The AM30 HD is treated by Vac2Go as essentially the same unit; see its own card.
