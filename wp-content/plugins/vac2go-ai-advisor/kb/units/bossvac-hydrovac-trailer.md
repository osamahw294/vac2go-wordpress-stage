# Bossvac Hydrovac Trailer

The BossVac BV-500, a towable hydro excavation trailer with its own engine, high pressure water pump, tri-lobe vacuum blower and a hydraulic tilting spoil tank.

## Key specs
- Length: 21.6 feet {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Width: 98.5 inches {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Height: 7.6 feet {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Weight (empty): 7,000 LB {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- GVWR: 14,000 LB {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Trailer axles: (2) 7000 LB {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Hitch: printed "BUMPER PULL OR PINAL" {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Engine: Yanmar, listed as "TIER 4"; horsepower printed as "36.8" {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Fuel tank: 17 gallons {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Water tank capacity: 400 gallons; number of tanks: 2 {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- High pressure pump: 4 GPM, 4000PSI {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Spoil tank: 800 gallons; 1/4" thick, "allowing for 15 PSI pressure off and full vacuum" {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Tank lift: hydraulic to 70 degree tilt {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Door: hydraulic, with 8 wing nut manual lock {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Vacuum: 500CFM, 24 inches (mercury), tri-lobe blower {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Filters: 5 micron {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}
- Hose: 30 feet length, 3 inches width {src: BossVac BV-500 spec sheet p.1}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation. The sheet names no specific job types.

## Standard and optional
- The sheet does not separate standard from optional equipment. The only stated choice is the hitch: bumper pull or the second type printed "PINAL". Also listed: internal wash out system.

## Not in our literature
- Blower make and model, water pump make, boom, water heater, tank material. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- "PINAL" is printed that way on the sheet (likely meaning pintle); a rep can confirm the hitch type on the unit being rented.
- "TIER 4" is an emissions tier, not an engine model; the engine model is not given. The horsepower figure has no unit printed.
- "Number of tanks: 2" sits with the 400 gallon water figure, and the sheet does not say whether 400 gallons is the total or per tank. Quote both as printed and do not interpret.
- The sheet is undated; a rep can confirm it matches Vac2Go's unit.
