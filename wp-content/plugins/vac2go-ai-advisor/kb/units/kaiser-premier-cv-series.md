# Kaiser Premier CV Series

The Kaiser Premier CV Series is a truck-mounted hydro excavator with a fixed (non-tipping) tank, a 6 way hydraulic boom and a PD vacuum blower.

## Models covered
Which configuration you get depends on the unit {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- CV100 (Tandem): 13.0 yd3 debris, 1,850 gal fresh water; 38' x 8'6" x 13'4" (L x W x H)
- CV100T (Tandem + Pusher Axle): 13.0 yd3 debris, 1,850 gal fresh water; 38' x 8'6" x 13'4"
- CV200 (Tri-Drive): 13.0 yd3 debris, 2,250 gal fresh water; 39'2" x 8'6" x 13'3"
- Tri-Drive + Pusher Axle (also labelled CV200): 13.0 yd3 debris, 2,250 gal fresh water; 39'2" x 8'6" x 13'3"

## Key specs
- Vacuum blower: Robuschi RB-DV 145, system capacity of 6,600 cfm and 27" Hg max. vacuum {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Other vacuum pump choices: Robuschi RB-DV 125, 3,500 cfm, 27" Hg; KAISER 7000i Liquid Ring, 4,120 cfm, 22" Hg {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Vacuum drive: direct drive via split shaft PTO (transfer case) {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Boom: 6 way hydraulic, 342-degree rotation, 26' reach or 20' down without adding an extension tube {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.2, p.5}
- Suction hose: 32' of 8" {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Final filter: 10 micron; hydraulic vacuum breaker {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Water pump: Cat Pumps 3560, 20 gpm and 3,000 psi (factory settings), variable speed hydraulic drive {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Dig hose reel: 75' of 1/2" hose, air power retract {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Tank: divided water and debris tanks, fixed tank (no tipping), sloped floor, no-tilt hydraulically controlled rear dump door {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4, p.5}
- Optional boiler: 700,000 BTU {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Optional flush: upgraded 102 gpm debris body flush (turbo flush) {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}
- Optional mechanically assisted offload: 11.7 - 12.8 yd3 tank capacity {src: Kaiser CV brochure p.4}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation. The brochure names no specific job list.
- Hot water digging with the optional boiler.
- Offloading without raising the tank, using the optional assisted offload.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: RB-DV 145 blower, split shaft PTO drive, 6 way boom with 8" suction hose, 10 micron final filter, Cat 3560 water pump, dig hose reel, wash bars in the tank and flush nozzle in the cyclone, wireless radio remote with back-up on-board controls, insulated heated aluminum van body, powder coated tanks.
- Optional: boiler, assisted offload, pusher, umbilical cord pendant, secondary digging hose reel, secondary steam hose reel, 100' of 1/2" hose, turbo flush, hydraulic trash pump, rear platforms, towing package, water system heat protection, weight scale system, air compressor. Other vacuum pumps (RB-DV 125, 7000i liquid ring).

## Not in our literature
- Chassis make, engine, weights, GVWR, dump angle (the tank does not tip). A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Capacities are listed as maximum capacity, and dimensions vary by chassis manufacturer.
- The assisted offload option reduces debris capacity to the 11.7 - 12.8 yd3 range.
- p.4 calls the water pump figures factory settings; p.5 calls them system capacity. Present them as factory settings.
- Which axle configuration and vacuum pump a unit has depends on the unit.
- The brochure dates from 2019.
