# Super Products Camel Max Series

The Super Products Camel Max Series are truck-mounted combination sewer cleaners (vacuum plus jetting), with an optional Hydro Excavation package.

## Models covered
Which model you get depends on the unit {src: Camel Max brochure p.2}
- Camel 900: 9 yards debris body; 1000 gallons water; Dump unloading; height 11' 6" (approximate)
- Camel 1200: 12 yards debris body; 1500 gallons water; Dump or Eject unloading; height 11' 9" (approximate)
- Camel 1600: 16 yards debris body; 2000 gallons water; Eject unloading; height 11' 9" (approximate)

## Key specs
- Vacuum blower (all models): positive displacement, 3600-5500 cfm; hot shift transfer case {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Dump unloading (900 and 1200): body raises up to a 50° angle {src: Camel Max brochure p.2}
- Eject unloading (1200 and 1600): body raises and the ejector plate pushes out debris; printed angle 10° {src: Camel Max brochure p.2}
- Cyclone: centrifugal, 44,000 in3 {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Debris body: 1/4" thick EXTEN steel; quick release door less than 30" off the ground {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Water pump: designed capacity of 100 gpm and 3000 psi {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Hose reel: front mounted; 270° rotation, 180° rotation fully retracted; capable of 1000' of 1" sewer hose; extends 18" {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Boom: 8" hydraulic with 8' hydraulic extension; 1000 lb lifting capacity; 217° rotation {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Tailgate: hydraulic, 4 adjustable hydraulic wedge pins {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}
- Optional Super Boom: 8" hose, 15' payout, 10' telescopic, 17-27' reach, 180° rotation {src: Camel Max brochure p.4}
- Optional water heater: 420,000 btu/hr {src: Camel Max brochure p.3}

## Typical uses
- Combination sewer cleaning.
- With the Hydro Excavation Package: cleaning sewer lines and digging holes with the same machine.
- With Wastewater Recycle (1200 and 1600 models): cleaning sewers without fresh water.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: single engine, poly water tank, air purge, antifreeze system, dual accumulators, dual hydraulic cooling, level gauge, debris tank flush-out, water pump sleep mode, controls to run the water pump with or without vacuum, backup camera, body up, boom up and tailgate alarms, safety props, safety cones, sewer hose containment shield.
- Optional: ACCULEVEL Load Sensor System, Hydro Excavation Package, powered level wind, High Dump (1200 models), hose reel tensioning, hydrant fill with auto shut off, lighting packages, chassis configurations, signboards, splash shield, Super Boom, tailgate tube rack, tool boxes, trash pump, Wastewater Recycle (1200 and 1600 models), water heater, winter recirculation, wireless remote.

## Not in our literature
- Vacuum rating in Hg, blower make and model, length, width, weights, GVWR, chassis make, High Dump height or specs. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The blower range is the same for all three models; the brochure does not say which unit gets which cfm.
- The eject angle is printed as "an 10°", which may be a misprint. Mention it only if asked, and say a rep can confirm.
- Water pump figures are the designed capacity; the rating actually delivered may differ.
- Heights are approximate.
- The brochure does not say whether a dump-configured 1200 can take Wastewater Recycle.
