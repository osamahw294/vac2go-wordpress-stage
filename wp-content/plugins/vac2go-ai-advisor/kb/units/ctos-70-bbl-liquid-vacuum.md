# CTOS 70-BBL Liquid Vacuum

A 70 barrel liquid vacuum truck in Vac2Go's Liquid Vacuum category.

## Not covered
- Pump or blower, tank material, certifications, dimensions and chassis. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- 70 barrels is the size class in the unit's name; do not convert it to gallons or add other figures.
- Do not borrow figures from the Huber Dominator, which is a different unit.
