# Huber King Vac

The Huber King Vac (formerly Keith Huber) is a liquid ring vacuum truck that combines deep vacuum with high air conveyance, for wet and dry material, with a dumping tank.

## Key specs
- Tank: 3000 Gallon (70 bbl) carbon steel tank with 5/16" shell {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Vacuum pump: 4120 CFM liquid ring pump with deep vacuum capabilities {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Pump drive: powered via transfer case and belt driven {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Intake: 6" intake valve with internal standpipe {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Discharge: 6" discharge valve {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Cyclone: cyclone technology with individual cyclone cleanout {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Wet and dry applications: hydro-excavation, hazmat response, jetting and general industrial cleaning, in the oil, gas and mining industries.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: hydraulically operated rear door and tank hoist with integrated safety valves, liquid level indicator, rear door and tank safety prop bars, rear work lights, LED running lights, back-up alarm, grounding reel.
- Optional: ASME/DOT 412 {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}; 20" manway {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}; Huber Lock rear door; stainless steel tank and wetted parts; auxiliary transfer pump; top mounted hydraulically operated 6" or 8" boom {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Optional wash down package: 10 gpm @ 2500 psi or 20 gpm @ 2500 psi, electric hose reel, 100' or 200' of 1/2" hose, hydrant water fill {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Optional jetting package: 35 gpm @ 2000 psi, electric or hydraulic hose reel, 1/2" to 3/4" jetting hose, integral water compartment {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}

## Not in our literature

## Notes for the advisor
- Single source: the King Vac brochure (copyright 1999-2017; the King Vac was introduced in 1986). The PDF is marked as a proof.
- Quote tank size as the brochure states it; do not do your own gallon/barrel conversions.
- Code (ASME/DOT 412), boom and water packages are options. Never say this unit has them without confirmation. {src: Huber_KingVac Brochure p.2}
- Do not count cyclones from photos; the brochure gives no number.
