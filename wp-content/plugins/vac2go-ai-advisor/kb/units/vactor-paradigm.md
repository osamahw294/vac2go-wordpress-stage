# Vactor Paradigm

The Vactor HXX Paradigm is a compact, single-engine vacuum excavator that can dig with water or air, aimed at utility, municipal and contractor work on tight job sites.

## Models covered
- One model, on a Class 6 non-CDL (state dependent) chassis or a Class 7 chassis (CDL required); 4X4 chassis available. Which chassis you get depends on the unit {src: Paradigm brochure p.5, p.8}

## Key specs
- Debris body: 675 gal volumetric capacity {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Water tank: 300 gal polyethylene {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Payload: more than 6,000 lbs on a non-CDL class 6 chassis; can be optioned up to 8200 lbs on a class 7 chassis; actual capacity depends on chassis configuration and tools stored {src: Paradigm brochure p.5, p.4}
- Blower: positive displacement, hydraulically driven, 2,200 CFM, 15" Hg {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Filtration: dual cyclone; final filter with washable 5-micron insert {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Boom: 6" diameter extendable, 5' of extension {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Dig depth: up to six feet without additional pipe and hose {src: Paradigm brochure p.3}
- Rear loading port: 4", with 4" x 25 ft vacuum hose {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Dump: hydraulically raising debris body, 48" unladed dump height {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Water pump: triplex, 8 GPM @ 2500 PSI {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Water hose reel: 1/2" x 50 ft {src: Paradigm brochure p.6}
- Air compressor: 185 CFM @ 150 PSI; 150 PSI for air excavation, 110 PSI for air tools {src: Paradigm brochure p.3, p.7}
- Air tool reel: 1" x 25 ft hose {src: Paradigm brochure p.7}
- Optional compressor upgrade: 300 CFM @ 250 PSI {src: Paradigm brochure p.8}
- Optional DigRight limits: 1500, 2500, 3000 PSI, or off {src: Paradigm brochure p.4}
- Optional backfill hopper: 175 U.S. gallon {src: Paradigm brochure p.8}
- Optional receiver or Pintle hitch: tows up to 20,000 lbs of additional equipment {src: Paradigm brochure p.3}

## Typical uses
- Excavating around water, sewer, gas, electric and telecom utilities; potholing, keyholing, slot trenching, daylighting.
- Fire hydrant and valve box repair, cathodic protection, cross-bore detection, fiber optic work, water main repair.
- Drill mud recovery, pit cleaning, spill cleanup, emergency response.
- Powering tools such as a jack hammer or tamper.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: everything above unless marked optional, plus wireless remote, IntelliView display, backup camera, E-stops and wrist strap, 6" air-operated vacuum relief valve.
- Optional: DigRight, backfill hopper, lockable storage, hitch, hydraulic tool package, cold weather protection, amber LED lights, LED work lighting, compressor upgrade, 4X4 or Class 7 chassis.

## Not in our literature
- Dimensions, weights, GVWR, dump angle, blower make and model, chassis make. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- Always give payload with its chassis caveat.
- Non-CDL status is state dependent. Never tell a customer they can drive it without a CDL; a rep confirms.
- The brochure also mentions Vactor's wider vacuum excavator range; those capacities are not the Paradigm's.
- The brochure dates from 2016/2017.
