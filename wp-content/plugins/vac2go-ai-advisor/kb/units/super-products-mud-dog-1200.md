# Super Products Mud Dog 1200

The Super Products Mud Dog 1200 is a 12 yard truck-mounted hydro excavator with a rear-mounted telescoping boom, heated water system and tilt-ejection unloading.

## Models covered
- One model, available on a tandem rear axle chassis, tandem rear axle with pusher axle, or tridem rear axle chassis. Which one you get depends on the unit {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}

## Key specs
- Debris body: 12 cu yd (9.1 m3) {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Water capacity: 1500 gal (5678 L) {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Water pump: 18 gpm and 3000 psi (68 lpm and 207 bar), hydraulic drive with variable flow on remote {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Water heater: 714,000 btu/hr (209 kw) {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Hose reel: 100' of 1/2" hose {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Vacuum system: 8" (20.3 cm) positive displacement, 5800 cfm/28" hg (9854 m3/hr/0.95 bar) {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Cyclone: externally mounted with 16" (406 mm) clean-out door; pleated final filter {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Boom: 8' (2.4 m) telescoping, 19' to 27' (5.8 m to 8.2 m) reach, 335° rotation, 45° upward and 25° downward pivot {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Unloading: ejector plate, tilt-ejection {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2, p.1}
- Float ball: 14" {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Controls: wired pendant with 50' (15.2 m) cord and wireless remote {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Cabinet heat: dual 65000 btu/hr (19 kw) heaters {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Tool box: 18" x 18" x 36" {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Optional sludge pump: 500 gpm (1893 lpm), rear door mounted {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}
- Optional liquid transfer pump: 300 gpm (1136 lpm), side mounted {src: Mud Dog 1200 brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation. The brochure names no specific job list.
- Working in dark conditions with the boom light package.

## Standard and optional
- Standard: single engine drive, CANbus controls, water heater, curbside water fill, winter recirculation, anti-freeze and air purge systems, internal body flush-out, hydraulic tailgate latches, full opening rear tailgate, LED and boom lighting packages, remote boom greasing.
- Optional: Acculevel Load Sensor System, sludge pump, liquid transfer pump, dual tire chain racks, safety cone rack, heated and insulated water supply and return lines.

## Not in our literature
- Dimensions, weights, GVWR, dump angle, dump height, blower make and model, chassis make. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The Acculevel badge appears on p.1, but p.2 lists Acculevel as optional. Treat it as optional.
