# Truvac HXX

The Truvac HXX is a truck-mounted hydro excavator (created by Vactor, formerly sold as the Vactor HXX) for mid-sized and full-sized excavation, using high pressure water or optional pressurized air. Figures are given for the current Truvac build and the earlier Vactor build.

## Models covered
Body size, vacuum system, water pump and boom are choices; which you get depends on the unit.
- Debris body: 12 yd3 or 15 yd3, 50° tilting {src: Truvac HXX sheet p.2}
- Vacuum (current Truvac build): positive displacement blower options rated 18 in-HG/4970 cfm, 28 in-HG/5200 cfm, or 28 in-HG/6176 cfm; also a two-stage centrifugal compressor {src: Truvac HXX sheet p.2}
- Vacuum (earlier Vactor build): Roots 824 (18 in Hg) 4970 cfm; Hibon 8702 (28 in Hg) 5250 cfm; Robuschi RBDV 145 (28 in Hg) 6,176 cfm; or a dual 38 in two-stage centrifugal compressor/fan system {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.6}

## Key specs
- Debris body (earlier Vactor build): 12 cubic yard standard, 15 cubic yard optional; 1/4 in Ex-Ten steel {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.5, p.7}
- Dump angle: 50 degree {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.5}
- PD blower drive: direct drive, heavy-duty transfer case {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.6}
- Boom (current Truvac build): 8" vacuum, extendable or telescopic, 22' reach, 320° rotation {src: Truvac HXX sheet p.2}
- Boom (earlier Vactor build): 7 ft top mounted hydraulic extendable boom, 22 ft (6.71 m) reach from the centerline of the truck {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.4, p.7}
- Vacuum hose: 8 in inside diameter {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.4}
- Water pump (current Truvac build): 10 or 20gpm at 3000 psi {src: Truvac HXX sheet p.2}
- Water pump (earlier Vactor build): standard 10 gpm @3000 PSI, optional 20 gpm {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.3, p.7}
- DigRight pressure limits: 1,500 PSI, 2,500 PSI, 3,000 PSI, or off {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.3}
- Handgun hose: 3/8 in x 75 ft on a retractable reel {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.3}
- Water tank (earlier Vactor build only): 1200 gallon HDPE with low level alarm {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.5}
- Optional water heater: 400,000 BTU/hr or 800,000 BTU/hr {src: Vactor HXX brochure p.7}

## Typical uses
- Precision excavation, exposing live gas distribution lines, utility location, daylighting, excavation in frozen ground, directional drilling mud recovery, pipe and line installation, tank cleaning, valve box clean out, drill rig cleanup, emergency response, water-line repair, slot trenching, potholing, pit cleaning, spill cleanup.
- Powering jack hammers or tampers; towing mini-excavators, HDD drills or job supply trailers.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (earlier Vactor build): 12 yd body, extendable boom, IntelliView, DigRight multi-flow water system, handgun hose reel, HDPE water tank, wireless remote, E-Stop.
- Optional (earlier Vactor build): 15 yd body, telescopic boom, 20 gpm pump, water heater, cold weather package, warming cabinet, hydraulic tool package, second operator station, extra and heated storage, stainless steel water tank, pressurized air excavation, fold-down pipe racks.

## Not covered
- Weights, GVWR, engine, chassis make, labelled dimensions, dump height; water tank capacity on the current build. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The 28 in Hg tri-lobe blower is listed at 5200 cfm on the current build and 5250 cfm (Hibon 8702) on the earlier build. Give both and say the exact figure depends on the unit's blower.
- 7 ft is the boom extension, 22 ft is the reach. Do not mix them.
- The 1200 gallon water tank is listed only for the earlier Vactor build.
