# GapVax HV-33

A smaller truck-mounted hydro excavator in the GapVax HV Series (also called the HV33 HydroVax), with a wet/dry filtration system for hydro excavation.

## Key specs
- Blower: 4,000 CFM, 27" Hg high vac blower {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Water tank: 600 gallon stainless steel {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Debris body: "6 Cu. Yd. Useable Stainless Steel Debris Body" {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Water pump (HX package): 12 GPM / 3,000 PSI, hydraulically driven {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Boom reach: 14'-18' from center of truck {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Boom rotation: 270° {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Boom height: 18' - 10" up from level {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Boom depth: 4' - 6" down from level {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Filtration: GapVax wet / dry filtration system (no cyclone count or filter detail stated) {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Tailgate: torque amplified tailgate locks {src: HV33 brochure p.2}
- Controls: all controls engage on the curbside {src: HV33 brochure p.2}

## Typical uses
- Hydro excavation, wet or dry.

## Standard and optional
- Standard (listed features): the blower, tanks, HX package, boom, tailgate locks and curbside controls above.
- Optional: on-board scales with digital readout that stop loading once a set target is reached; heated pump box with water heater; pump-off system. {src: HV33 brochure p.2}

## Not covered
- Blower make and model, hose sizes and lengths, overall dimensions, weights/GVWR, chassis, engine, axle configuration, water heater BTU rating, and gross (as opposed to useable) debris capacity. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these.

## Notes for the advisor
- The HV33 is an older GapVax model. Give the figures as stated, with the configuration note, and a rep can confirm the current unit's build.
- The debris figure is "useable" capacity; quote it with that word.
- Do not carry HV-56 or other HV Series figures over to the HV-33 (for example, the HV-33 blower is listed at 27" Hg, not 28" Hg).
- A water heater is optional; do not say the rental unit has one unless a rep confirms.
- Printed specs are subject to change.
