# Kaiser Premier TerraVac

The Kaiser Premier TerraVac Xpose800 is a trailer-mounted hydro excavator in a compact footprint, for municipal work and smaller or hard to access jobs.

## Models covered
- Our literature covers the TerraVac Xpose800 only {src: TerraVac brochure p.1, p.2}

## Key specs
- Length: 21' {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Width: 8' 6" {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Height: 8' 8" {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Debris tank: 800 gallons, 1/4" steel, with a welded stainless-steel floor {src: TerraVac brochure p.2, p.3}
- Fresh water: 400 gallons {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Engine: 49-Hp diesel {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Water: up to 4,000 psi at 4.5 gpm {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Blower: 960 cfm of suction, 15" Hg vacuum, 5 psi positive pressure {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Suction hose: 30', 3" diameter; attaches to the 4" loading port with a 3" reducer {src: TerraVac brochure p.2, p.3}
- Suction extensions: 4' and 6' long, included {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Digging tools: 6' dig lance and 36" wash gun {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Water hose reel: 50', 3/8" diameter {src: TerraVac brochure p.3}
- Offload: 6" dumping port for positive pressure offloading; 6" drain port with knife gate valve {src: TerraVac brochure p.2, p.3}
- Dump door: central locking bar, without grabbing claws or internal hydraulics {src: TerraVac brochure p.2}
- Tank: tips, from a Tank Tipping Up/Down switch on the control panel {src: TerraVac brochure p.3}

## Typical uses
- Potholing, daylighting and material reclamation.
- Municipal jobs and smaller or hard to access sites.
- Cutting through soil and clay.

## Standard and optional
- Included: suction hose, both extensions, dig lance, wash gun, hose reel, loading port reducer, drain port, door locking mechanism, water pump ignition reset (pump shuts off with the engine).
- Optional: glycol tank.

## Not in our literature
- GVWR, empty weight, axle rating, hitch or coupler type, brakes, required tow vehicle, blower type, engine make, dump angle. A Vac2Go rep can confirm these, and must confirm towing requirements before a customer tows it.

## Notes for the advisor
- The brochure links the TerraVac to the CV Series, but its figures are much smaller. Do not imply CV Series performance.
- Do not describe the debris tank as stainless; only the floor is.
- Specs come from the manufacturer brochure (footer code 12012020) and may have changed.
