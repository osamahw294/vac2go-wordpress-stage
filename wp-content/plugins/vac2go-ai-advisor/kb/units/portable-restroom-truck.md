# Portable Restroom Truck

A portable toilet service truck: a stainless steel two-compartment tank (waste plus fresh water), a vacuum pump with suction hoses and wands, a wash down pump, and a rear carrier with hitch for hauling portable toilets.

## Key specs
Figures come from the build specification for this truck.
- Tank: 304 stainless steel, two compartments, 1,600 US gallons total, waste and fresh water {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.1}
- Fresh water compartment: front of tank, approximate capacity 500 US gallons {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.1}
- Waste compartment: rear of tank, approximate capacity 1,100 US gallons {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.1}
- Tank size: outside diameter of 62" and shell length of 110" {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.1}
- Vacuum pump: National Vacuum Equipment 304, air cooled, shaft driven (PTO), 180 CFM peak air flow {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.4}
- Suction hoses: two 2" x 50' suction hoses with 2" plastic valves and wands, one each side {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.9}
- Upper suctions: dual 3" upper suctions, reduced to 2" poly valves and cam-lock fittings {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.2}
- Waste outlet: 4" stainless outlet, bottom rear of waste compartment, 4" brass ball valve and cam-lock adapter {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.2-3}
- Fresh water pump: 12 volt AMT model 12DC-98, stainless steel, electric wash down pump {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.5}
- Garden hose reel: spring rewind, holds 50' of 1/2" garden hose {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.5}
- Toilet carrier hitch: rated for 8000#, 18" ground height; 7-way RV style light plug; in-cab brake controller {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.7}
- Toolboxes: roadside and curbside, 25D x 27H x 36W stainless steel {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.3-4}
- Chassis: Mack MD 6, model year 2025; wheelbase 175" {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.6}
- Axle ratings: front GAWR 10K; rear GAWR 19K {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.6}
- Engine / transmission: Cummins ISB 6.7L; Allison 2500 RDS {src: Portable Restroom Truck build spec p.6}

## Typical uses
- Servicing portable toilets: pumping waste, refilling fresh water, washing down, and carrying toilets on the rear carrier or towing a trailer.

## Standard and optional
- The build spec lists one configuration and does not separate standard from optional. Also listed: two 5" sight eyes on the waste compartment, fresh water sight tube, stainless hose troughs, roadside work station, LED work and clearance lights, backup alarm, two backup camera systems, bucket and spray bottle holders.
