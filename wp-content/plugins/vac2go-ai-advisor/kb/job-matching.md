== WHICH TRUCK FOR WHICH JOB (Vac2Go's own answers, covering the whole fleet) ==
From Vac2Go's Round 1 Knowledge Base Entries, Groups B and C. Use these to match a job or an industry to a category; the category's own knowledge and unit cards give the detail.

### Group B: Application & Job Matching

**Q: I need to clean out a sludge pit: what truck do I need?**

For most sludge pits, a Liquid Vacuum Truck (LVPD, LVTS or LVT) is the best choice. It is designed to remove sludge, slurry, heavy solids, and wet debris from industrial pits, sumps, and process areas.

**Q: I need to vacuum dry industrial dust: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is typically the right solution. It can recover dry powders, dust, fly ash, cement dust, and other fine industrial materials efficiently.

**Q: I need to recover spilled aggregate or pellets: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is generally the best option. It can quickly recover sand, gravel, plastic pellets, grain, catalyst, and other bulk materials while minimizing product loss and cleanup time

**Q: I have a spill at an industrial facility: what truck do I need?**

It depends on the material:
- Industrial vacuum truck for non-Hazard, solids and mixed material.
- Liquid vacuum truck if the spill is primarily liquid.
If you’re unsure, our team can recommend the best equipment based on the material being recovered.

**Q: I need to clean a catch basin: what truck do I need?**

For most catch basin cleaning, a combination sewer cleaner is the preferred choice because it combines high-pressure water jetting with vacuum recovery. If heavy debris removal is the primary task, a liquid vacuum truck may also be appropriate.

**Q: I need to clean silt traps at a ready-mix plant: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is usually the best choice for removing heavy silt, slurry, concrete fines, and accumulated material from ready-mix operations.

**Q: I need to clean out a storage tank: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is commonly used for storage tank cleanouts involving sludge, solids, and residual products. If the tank contains only pumpable liquid, a liquid vacuum truck may be more appropriate.

**Q: I need to vacuum out a silo or bin: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is the preferred solution for removing dry bulk materials, powders, grain, cement, or accumulated product from silos and storage bins.

**Q: I have wet pool filter sand that needs to be removed: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is ideal for removing saturated filter media such as wet sand, gravel, or similar materials that are difficult to remove manually.

**Q: I need to vacuum a 25-foot deep hole for a hydraulic elevator replacement: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is often the best choice for removing soil, wet material and debris from deep elevator pits where excavation has already occurred and efficient material removal is needed. For water or slurry, a combination unit or hydro excavator is the better choice.

**Q: I need to excavate work pits for electrical conduit tie-ins: what truck do I need?**

A hydro excavator is the safest and most efficient choice. It uses pressurized water and vacuum to excavate around existing underground utilities while minimizing the risk of damage.

**Q: I have a micro-trenching project: what truck do I need?**

An Industrial Vacuum Truck is the most commonly used system when micro-trenching with an attached micro-trencher

**Q: I need to vacuum around a water main: what truck do I need?**

A hydro excavator is recommended because it allows for safe, non-destructive excavation around buried water mains and other utilities.

**Q: I need to do daylighting around buried utilities: what truck do I need?**

A hydro excavator is specifically designed for daylighting and utility locating. It safely exposes underground infrastructure using water cutting or air knifing along with vacuum power instead of mechanical digging.

**Q: I need to vacuum cement and concrete debris at a job site: what truck do I need?**

An industrial vacuum truck is generally the best option for recovering cement dust, concrete fines, slurry, and other construction debris from industrial or commercial job sites.

**Q: I need to pump out and clean a storm drain: what truck do I need?**

A combination sewer cleaner is typically the right choice because it can both jet the line and vacuum the debris, providing a complete storm drain cleaning solution.

**Q: I need to recover used cooking oil from restaurants: what truck do I need?**

A liquid vacuum truck is the appropriate choice for collecting and transporting used cooking oil and other pumpable liquid waste.

**Q: I need to vacuum out grease traps: what truck do I need?**

A liquid vacuum truck is typically the best solution for grease trap service because it is designed to remove pumpable grease, wastewater, and organic waste efficiently.

**Q: I need to support a refinery shutdown / turnaround: what truck do I need?**

In most cases, an industrial vacuum truck is the preferred choice for refinery turnarounds. These projects often involve cleaning process equipment, tanks, pits, catalyst removal, and recovering industrial solids, sludge, and debris. Depending on the scope of work, Liquid Rings, Scrubbers or liquid vacuum trucks may also be used to support specific tasks.
